    LOCAL EXPERIMENT / AES-256-GCM

    Seal a message. Verify it survived.

    Real authenticated encryption inside your browser. Keys and plaintext are not sent to the server or saved by this app. This lab does not transmit over radio or configure your radio's keys.

    A fresh random 96-bit nonce is generated for every message. Keep the key separate from the envelope. This lab has no key exchange or replay protection.

    Generate a key to begin. Refreshing clears the session.
