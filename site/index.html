Let the Pi receive.
Let your screen shine.

The Pi 3 has 1 GB of RAM. SignalPier uses a small Python server and native receiver tools, while your laptop or phone draws the maps and charts.

No desktop environment, containers, or Node.js are required on the Pi. Captures are limited, plotted data is capped, and the receiver handles one job at a time.
